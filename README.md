# About

The **PowerChain Whitepaper** is the authoritative technical, economic and governance specification for the PowerChain ecosystem.

It documents the principles, architecture and operational model of the protocol while defining the design decisions behind the PWRC digital asset and the broader infrastructure supporting renewable energy, environmental markets and tokenised real-world assets.

This repository is maintained alongside protocol development and evolves through the **PowerChain Improvement Proposal (PIP)** governance process.

---

# Vision

PowerChain aims to establish a secure, programmable financial infrastructure for the global energy transition.

The protocol enables renewable energy assets, environmental commodities and infrastructure-backed financial products to be issued, managed and settled on modern blockchain infrastructure.

---
